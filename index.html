Hello 👋 my name is Jakob, and I work as a mid-level engineer at Cvent.

My passion for software began at the ripe old age of 17, and it started, like any good story, with learning how to create a "guess the number" game in C++.

Since then, my mind has been captured by all of the potential that lives within these simple characters we render onto a screen.

So, I thought, why not try using plain vanilla techniques for building my own website? And that is exactly what I did.

Even though I find joy and efficiency in using LLM tools like OpenCode for development at work, I've discovered that there's nothing like writing the code yourself, and making mistakes along the way, in order to really learn something.

As much as I love to sit and type, I have a few other hobbies that (I like to think) diversify those sparse neural synapsis inside my brain.

These include hiking in the beautiful Utah mountains and cozying up to a good book, typically with a cup of coffee in hand.

I hope you enjoy perusing this site even a fraction as much as I enjoyed creating it.
