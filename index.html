Hello 👋 my name is Jakob, and I work as a mid-level engineer at Cvent.

My passion for software began at the ripe old age of 17, and it started, like any good story, with learning how to create a "guess the number" game in C++.

Since then, my mind has been captured by all of the potential that lives within these simple characters we render on to a screen.

So, I thought, why not try using plain vanilla techniques for building my own website? And that is exactly what I did.

As much as I love to sit and type, I have a few other hobbies that (I like to think) diversify those sparse neural synapsis inside my brain.
